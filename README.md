RevoFlux is a premier full-cycle software engineering and enterprise automation agency. We architect, build, and deploy mission-critical software solutions—ranging from multi-tenant cloud ERPs, dispatch & logistics CRMs, and healthcare portals to autonomous AI process automation—for clients across North America, the UK, and Europe.

## 🚀 Flagship Full-Stack Systems & Open Architectures

### 💼 Enterprise Business & Financial Platforms
- 🏢 **[`revoflux-fullstack-erp`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-fullstack-erp)**: Multi-tenant ERP, billing, and general ledger suite. *(Next.js 15, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL 16, Docker)*
- 💳 **[`revoflux-subscription-saas`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-subscription-saas)**: Complete B2B multi-tenant SaaS foundation with automated Stripe recurring billing and team organizations. *(Next.js 15, Stripe, Prisma, PostgreSQL)*

### 🚚 Logistics, Supply Chain & Retail Systems
- 🚛 **[`revoflux-fleet-logistics-crm`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-fleet-logistics-crm)**: Real-time freight dispatch, GPS telematics, and electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD) command center. *(React, FastAPI, PostGIS, Docker)*
- 🛒 **[`revoflux-smart-pos`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-smart-pos)**: Cloud-native Point of Sale (POS) terminal with offline-first caching and ESC/POS thermal printing. *(Next.js 15, TypeScript)*
- 📦 **[`revoflux-inventory-wms`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-inventory-wms)**: Multi-warehouse stock tracking, bin/rack management, and automated reorder alerts. *(FastAPI, Python, Pydantic v2)*

### 🏥 Healthcare & Dedicated Industry Portals
- 🩺 **[`revoflux-health-clinic-portal`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-health-clinic-portal)**: HIPAA-aligned clinic management, electronic health records (EHR), and patient telehealth portal. *(Next.js 15, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Docker)*

### 🤖 Intelligent Automation & AI Workflows
- ⚙️ **[`revoflux-business-automations`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-business-automations)**: Production-ready SMB automation workflows (WhatsApp order alerts, AI lead enrichment, automated invoice generation). *(n8n, Python, Webhooks)*
- 🧠 **[`revoflux-ai-agent-engine`](https://github.com/revoflux/revoflux-ai-agent-engine)**: Autonomous multi-agent B2B intelligence and lead qualification pipeline. *(Python, FastAPI)*
